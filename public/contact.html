Reporting a bug

To help us fix it quickly, please include:

  • Your device and browser (for example "Android, Chrome" or "iPhone, Safari")
  • The mode you were playing (Daily, Classic, Adventure level number, etc.)
  • What happened, and what you expected to happen
  • A screenshot, if you can

Deleting your data

Most of your progress is stored only in your browser. Clearing this site's data removes it. If you created a profile or appear on a leaderboard and want that removed, email us your profile code or display name and the date you played, and we will delete it. See the privacy policy for details.

Leaderboard problems

Every daily score is verified by replaying your moves on our server. If a score you earned didn't appear, or you see an offensive display name on a board, let us know the date and the name.

Partnerships and press

For game-portal distribution, licensing, sponsorship or press enquiries, use the same email address with a short subject line saying what it's about.
