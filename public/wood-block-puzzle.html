Wood block puzzle, free in your browser

Timberblock's Timber look turns the classic 8×8 puzzle into a set of real wooden blocks.

Play Timberblock free
To play with wooden blocks: open Timberblock, scroll to Look on the home screen and choose Timber. Your choice is remembered.

Same game, three looks

The rules are identical whichever look you choose:

  • Timber: wooden blocks on a maple bench
  • Voltage: neon arcade blocks on dark glass
  • Chapel: jewel-toned stained glass set in lead

Why play Timberblock instead of an app?

  • No download, no sign-up. It opens instantly in any browser on phone, tablet or computer.
  • Works offline after your first visit, and you can add it to your home screen.
  • A daily board that everyone shares, so you can compare scores with friends.
  • Challenge links that send a friend the exact same game.

How to play

Drag blocks from the tray onto the 8×8 board. Fill a whole row or column to clear it. The game ends when none of your blocks fit. Full rules and scoring are on the how to play page.
